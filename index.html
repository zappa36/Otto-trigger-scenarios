📞 TAKE A CALL FROM OTTO

Otto rings you from the back office. You answer as the person called — the customer, or the driver — and say what you like: not home before six, running late, fine. The call is saved like a report; the dashboard's CALLS tab lists it under the call.

How to play: three times, Otto says "two", keeps a silent beat, then says "four" — and you say "stop". Side: on two for left, in the silence for the middle, on four for right. Height: two is the top, the silence the middle, four the bottom. Strength: the silence is just right; two falls short, four flies over. Say nothing and he takes four. Stop in the silence three times for a bullseye; a miss is nothing. Otto explains this himself the first three times.
